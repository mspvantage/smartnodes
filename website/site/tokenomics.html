The SNODE token

Draft. Please read first.

Everything on this page describes the settings of the SmartNodes test network. They are placeholders, still to be modelled, reviewed by lawyers and decided, and they may all change.

SNODE is not for sale, and nothing here is an offer of it, or financial, legal or investment advice. SNODE is designed to be used on the network: to bond nodes, secure the chain and pay fees. No one should expect it to make money for them. On the test network it has no value at all.

The final settings will be published before any network with real value starts.

The basics

  • SNODE is the token of the SmartNodes chain. Its smallest unit is the usnode: 1 SNODE = 1,000,000 usnode.
  • Users do not need SNODE to use the VPN: they hold tickets, which hide who bought them. The ticket price is set per issuer key; on the test network it is 1 SNODE per ticket.

New SNODE is issued at a fixed 2% a year to pay validators and stakers, and stops for good at the cap. Unlike many Cosmos chains, the rate does not rise when less is staked.

Early operators can be sponsored: a sponsor pool lends them the bond, which stays locked and slashable, and goes back to the pool, not to the operator, when the node leaves. At most 500 nodes can be sponsored at once.

Where ticket payments go

When a node redeems a ticket, the chain pays the operator the ticket's price less a 5% protocol fee. The fee is divided:

50%to validators and the people staking with them
30%to the community pool, spent only by governance vote
20%to the prober fund, which pays for testing nodes

Tickets that are never spent expire with their issuer key, and their value goes to the community pool.

Node rewards

Besides ticket income, a node reward pool pays nodes for passing verification. It deliberately does not pay for ticket volume, which an operator could fake by spending tickets at their own node.

  • Each year the pool releases about 20% of what it still holds.
  • 80% of each release goes to nodes that pass, and 20% to probers.
  • A node can earn at most 30% of its tier's bond in a year this way.
  • How often a node is tested does not change what it can earn over time.
  • How the pool is filled at the start is not decided. Anyone can add to it.

Validators

  • At most 30 validators.
  • Equal share: half of validator income (fees, the stakers' share of ticket fees, and new issue) is paid equally to every validator whose operator stakes at least 5,000 SNODE of their own with it. The other half is paid by stake, as usual. This stops the largest validators growing just because they are largest.
  • Commission between 5% and 20%. The ceiling is fixed when a validator is created and can never be raised.
  • 2% of staking rewards go to the community pool.
  • Non-voting stake: listed accounts, such as a treasury or team holdings, still stake and earn but get no say in governance votes, so that holding tokens does not mean controlling the rules.
